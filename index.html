Healthcare RAG AssistantDEPI capstone · Team of five

Answers only when the evidence is there.

A health Q&A assistant that retrieves, reranks and cites its sources, and declines to answer when the evidence is weak.

Watch the demoView on GitHub

How a question becomes an answer.

01SafetyLocal rules, then a Gemini safety check.
02RetrieveBGE-M3 embeddings, FAISS top 40.
03RerankA cross-encoder scores each passage.
04DecideThe best score has to clear a calibrated cutoff.
Strong evidenceAnswerGemini answers from the passages, and every citation ID is validated.
Weak evidenceAbstainNo answer when evidence is weak or a citation fails.
92%Abstention precision. 11 of the 12 answers it withheld should have been withheld.
79%Abstention recall. It declined 11 of the 14 questions it should decline.
100%Valid citation IDs. All 48 answers cite only passages that were supplied.
5 of 8Policy-scope abstention. The weakest spot, and the one I’d fix first.

Evaluated on 87 questions with an LLM judge (gpt-oss-120b, rubric outcomes-v4), so treat the figures as estimates, not verified accuracy. Educational project; not a medical device and not clinically validated.
